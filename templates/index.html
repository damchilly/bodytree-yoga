About

A studio grown from stillness and soil.

BodyTree Yoga began with a simple belief: the body is a living tree — flexible when tended, resilient when rooted. Our practice blends classical alignment with slow, nature-inspired sequencing so every student can feel grounded without rushing toward perfection.

Whether you are unrolling a mat for the first time or returning after years away, you will find soft light, clear cueing, and a community that favors presence over performance.
